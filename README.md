# law

What the universe is, through cables and mathematics and physics: gravity, electrons, photons,
electromagnetic forces. The laws any system modelled here must be checked against.

Open `index.html`. Everything is collapsed. Open what you want.

## The format, which is the point

Every law is written in five parts, in this order, always:

1. **The law**, in one sentence, with no symbols.
2. **What it means**, in plain words.
3. **The maths**, with every symbol named. Not "where E is the field" but what E, r, ln and the
   square root sign each actually are.
4. **A worked number**, from a cable, a cell or a network.
5. **How to show it is wrong.** A law that cannot be falsified is not a law, it is an opinion.

**Notation is the last step and never the price of entry.** Anyone who has sized a cable, checked
a volt drop or read a derating table has been doing this mathematics for years under real
consequences. What is usually missing is the notation, and notation is a teaching problem, not a
capability one. So the notation is explained here rather than assumed, every time, including the
symbols that textbooks treat as too obvious to mention.

## The laws so far

| | law | falsified by |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | One photon frees one electron, or none | current from silicon beyond 1107 nm |
| **L2** | A fixed photon supply sets a maximum current | a silicon cell above 44.8 mA/cm² at one sun |
| **L3** | Excess photon energy becomes heat, which sets the ceiling | single junction silicon above 29.4% |
| **L4** | Stress is highest at the conductor surface | a stress peak anywhere else in a concentric cable |
| **L5** | Alternating current avoids the middle of a thick conductor | 1000 mm² solid with AC resistance equal to DC |
| **L6** | Circles pack tightest as a honeycomb | a plane packing denser than 0.9069 |
| **L7** | Heat rises with the square of the current | heating proportional to current in fixed resistance |
| **L8** | Every metre takes voltage away | no difference end to end under load |
| **L9** | What flows in flows out | an unbalanced junction with everything accounted for |
| **L10** | Three phases carry more with less metal | the √3 absent from measured three phase power |
| **L11** | Everything you see is the past | information carried faster than c |
| **L12** | Past a certain crowding, things cannot be told apart | counting correctly beyond the confusion limit |
| **L13** | Whatever you look at, everything else still exists | any frame where the three parts do not sum |

## Still to write

Gravity. Pauli exclusion, which is why matter takes up space and is not a force at all.
Faraday and Lenz, and therefore transformers. Thermodynamics and where efficiency ceilings
actually come from. Fault current and let-through energy. Carrier diffusion and recombination
between L1 and L2, which is the step that decides whether a freed electron is ever collected.
The crystallographic restriction, which is why you cannot have both a lattice and isotropy, and
which decides how anything may be drawn.
